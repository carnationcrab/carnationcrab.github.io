Last Light

A browser survival puzzle project to explore game-state logic, and pacing. Smaller in scope than Nova Roll, because when we evaluated the games created in our games project, nova roll was by far the most demanding game to run. So, I sought to make the lightest game to run. This one I tried to be a little more intentional about atmosphere than I was in nova roll.
